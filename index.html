Introduction

Collected here are three essays that argue against a design history made up of a roster of masters and masterpieces, and maintain that ordinary, anonymous printed matter is design history too. All three hold that design should be understood from different perspectives and dimensions. A ration book with no author, for example, can tell us as much about the design and daily life of its time as a poster by a famous designer. This website, then, is itself an unsigned collection of ration tickets. I drew on the colors of this kind of printed matter from three countries, and the ways it was used, for the overall style of the site, so that the site and the essays echo each other.
